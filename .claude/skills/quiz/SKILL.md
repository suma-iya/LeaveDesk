name: quiz
description: Interview practice on this codebase. Use when I say quiz me or test me.
---
Ask me 5 questions, one at a time, like a technical interviewer at a software company.
Cover: request flow, JWT auth, role checks, database layer, Docker/nginx setup.
Wait for each answer, grade it (correct / partly / wrong), give the ideal answer in 2-3 sentences, then ask the next question.
Finish with a score and my weakest topic.
